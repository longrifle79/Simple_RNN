Full Backpropagation Example

Forward Propagation + Complete Backpropagation (Updating Both Weights)

Goal: This version shows the complete backpropagation process on our tiny network, updating both \( w_{i \to h} \) and \( w_{h_1 \to o} \) after every training example, with full textbook-style derivative expansions.

Given Values

  • Input: \( x = 0.5 \)
  • Initial weight (input → hidden): \( w_{i \to h} = 0.4 \)
  • Initial weight (hidden → output): \( w_{h_1 \to o} = 0.7 \)
  • Target: \( t = 0.8 \)
  • Learning rate: \( \alpha = 0.5 \)

Example 1: Input = 0.5, Target = 0.8

Weights before this example: \( w_{i \to h} = 0.4 \), \( w_{h_1 \to o} = 0.7 \)

Forward Propagation

1 Compute hidden_net
\[\text{hidden\_net} = w_{i \to h} \times x = 0.4 \times 0.5 = 0.2000\]
2 Apply sigmoid to get hidden output \( o_{h_1} \)
\[o_{h_1} = \sigma(0.2000) = \frac{1}{1 + e^{-0.2}} \approx 0.5498\]
3 Compute output_net
\[\text{output\_net} = w_{h_1 \to o} \times o_{h_1} = 0.7 \times 0.5498 \approx 0.3849\]
4 Apply sigmoid to get final output \( o \)
\[o = \sigma(0.3849) \approx 0.5951\]
5 Compute error \( E \)
\[E = (t - o)^2 = (0.8 - 0.5951)^2 \approx 0.0420\]

Backpropagation – Hidden → Output Weight (\( w_{h_1 \to o} \))

\[\frac{\partial E}{\partial w_{h_1 \to o}} = \frac{\partial E}{\partial o} \cdot \frac{\partial o}{\partial \text{output\_net}} \cdot \frac{\partial \text{output\_net}}{\partial w_{h_1 \to o}}\]
A How error changes with the output
\[E = (t - o)^2\]
\[\frac{\partial E}{\partial o} = \frac{\partial}{\partial o}(t - o)^2 = 2(t - o) \cdot \frac{\partial}{\partial o}(t - o)\]
\[\frac{\partial}{\partial o}(t - o) = -1 \quad \Rightarrow \quad \frac{\partial E}{\partial o} = -2(t - o) \approx -0.4098\]
B How output changes with output_net (Sigmoid derivative)
\[\frac{\partial o}{\partial \text{output\_net}} = o(1 - o) \approx 0.5951 \times (1 - 0.5951) \approx 0.2410\]
C How output_net depends on the weight
\[\text{output\_net} = w_{h_1 \to o} \times o_{h_1} \quad \Rightarrow \quad \frac{\partial \text{output\_net}}{\partial w_{h_1 \to o}} = o_{h_1} \approx 0.5498\]
Gradient for \( w_{h_1 \to o} \) ≈ −0.0543
Updated weight: \( w_{h_1 \to o} = 0.7 + 0.5 \times 0.0543 \approx \mathbf{0.7272} \)

Backpropagation – Input → Hidden Weight (\( w_{i \to h} \))

We now continue the chain rule backward through the hidden node to find how the error depends on \( w_{i \to h} \).

\[\frac{\partial E}{\partial w_{i \to h}} = \frac{\partial E}{\partial o} \cdot \frac{\partial o}{\partial \text{output\_net}} \cdot \frac{\partial \text{output\_net}}{\partial o_{h_1}} \cdot \frac{\partial o_{h_1}}{\partial \text{hidden\_net}} \cdot \frac{\partial \text{hidden\_net}}{\partial w_{i \to h}}\]
D How output_net depends on hidden output \( o_{h_1} \)
\[\text{output\_net} = w_{h_1 \to o} \times o_{h_1} \quad \Rightarrow \quad \frac{\partial \text{output\_net}}{\partial o_{h_1}} = w_{h_1 \to o} \approx 0.7\]
E How hidden output changes with hidden_net (Sigmoid derivative)
\[\frac{\partial o_{h_1}}{\partial \text{hidden\_net}} = o_{h_1}(1 - o_{h_1}) \approx 0.5498 \times 0.4502 \approx 0.2475\]
F How hidden_net depends on the input weight
\[\text{hidden\_net} = w_{i \to h} \times x \quad \Rightarrow \quad \frac{\partial \text{hidden\_net}}{\partial w_{i \to h}} = x = 0.5\]
Gradient for \( w_{i \to h} \) ≈ −0.0086
Updated weight: \( w_{i \to h} = 0.4 + 0.5 \times 0.0086 \approx \mathbf{0.4043} \)

Example 2: Input = 0.2, Target = 0.4

Weights before this example: \( w_{i \to h} \approx 0.4043 \), \( w_{h_1 \to o} \approx 0.7272 \)

Forward Propagation (Summary)

hidden_net ≈ 0.0809 → \( o_{h_1} \approx 0.5202 \)
output_net ≈ 0.3784 → \( o \approx 0.5935 \)
Error \( E \approx 0.0374 \)

Backpropagation – Both Weights

Gradient for \( w_{h_1 \to o} \) ≈ +0.0484
Updated: \( w_{h_1 \to o} \approx \mathbf{0.7030} \)
Gradient for \( w_{i \to h} \) ≈ +0.0031
Updated: \( w_{i \to h} \approx \mathbf{0.4028} \)

Example 3: Input = 0.8, Target = 0.9

Weights before this example: \( w_{i \to h} \approx 0.4028 \), \( w_{h_1 \to o} \approx 0.7030 \)

Forward Propagation (Summary)

hidden_net ≈ 0.3222 → \( o_{h_1} \approx 0.5799 \)
output_net ≈ 0.4077 → \( o \approx 0.6005 \)
Error \( E \approx 0.0897 \)

Backpropagation – Both Weights

Gradient for \( w_{h_1 \to o} \) ≈ −0.0834
Updated: \( w_{h_1 \to o} \approx \mathbf{0.7447} \)
Gradient for \( w_{i \to h} \) ≈ −0.0109
Updated: \( w_{i \to h} \approx \mathbf{0.4083} \)

Final Weights After One Full Epoch

Weight Initial After Ex. 1 After Ex. 2 Final (After Ex. 3)
\( w_{i \to h} \) 0.4000 0.4043 0.4028 0.4083

Key Observations:

  • Both weights are updated after every single example.
  • The gradient (and therefore the update) for \( w_{i \to h} \) is smaller because the error signal travels through more layers.
  • This is the complete backpropagation algorithm working on a small network.
