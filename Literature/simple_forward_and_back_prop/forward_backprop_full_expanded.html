Simple Neural Network — Full Backpropagation

Forward + Complete Backpropagation (updating both weights)

Purpose: This is the most complete version of the tiny network example. We now perform full backpropagation and update both weights after every training example, exactly as it is done in real neural networks.

Given Values

  • Input: \( x = 0.5 \)
  • Initial \( w_{i \to h} = 0.4 \)
  • Initial \( w_{h_1 \to o} = 0.7 \)
  • Target: \( t = 0.8 \)
  • Learning rate: \( \alpha = 0.5 \)

Example 1: \( x = 0.5 \), target \( t = 0.8 \)

Weights before update: \( w_{i \to h} = 0.4 \), \( w_{h_1 \to o} = 0.7 \)

Forward Propagation

1 hidden_net = 0.4 × 0.5 = 0.2000
2 \( o_{h_1} = \sigma(0.2000) \approx \mathbf{0.5498} \)
3 output_net = 0.7 × 0.5498 ≈ 0.3849
4 \( o = \sigma(0.3849) \approx \mathbf{0.5951} \)
5 \( E = (0.8 - 0.5951)^2 \approx \mathbf{0.0420} \)

Backpropagation — Hidden → Output Weight

\[\frac{\partial E}{\partial w_{h_1 \to o}} = \frac{\partial E}{\partial o} \cdot \frac{\partial o}{\partial z_k} \cdot \frac{\partial z_k}{\partial w_{h_1 \to o}}\]
A How error changes with output
\[E = (t - o)^2 \quad \Rightarrow \quad \frac{\partial E}{\partial o} = -2(t - o) \approx -0.4098\]
B Sigmoid derivative
\[\frac{\partial o}{\partial z_k} = o(1 - o) \approx 0.2410\]
C How net input depends on weight
\[\frac{\partial z_k}{\partial w_{h_1 \to o}} = o_{h_1} \approx 0.5498\]
Gradient for \( w_{h_1 \to o} \) ≈ −0.0543
Updated: \( w_{h_1 \to o} = 0.7 + 0.5 \times 0.0543 \approx \mathbf{0.7272} \)

Backpropagation — Input → Hidden Weight (Full Chain)

We now continue the chain rule backward through the hidden node.

\[\frac{\partial E}{\partial w_{i \to h}} = \frac{\partial E}{\partial o} \cdot \frac{\partial o}{\partial z_k} \cdot \frac{\partial z_k}{\partial o_{h_1}} \cdot \frac{\partial o_{h_1}}{\partial z_h} \cdot \frac{\partial z_h}{\partial w_{i \to h}}\]
D How output_net depends on hidden activation
\[z_k = w_{h_1 \to o} \cdot o_{h_1} \quad \Rightarrow \quad \frac{\partial z_k}{\partial o_{h_1}} = w_{h_1 \to o} \approx 0.7\]
E Sigmoid derivative at hidden node
\[\frac{\partial o_{h_1}}{\partial z_h} = o_{h_1}(1 - o_{h_1}) \approx 0.2475\]
F How hidden_net depends on input weight
\[z_h = w_{i \to h} \cdot x \quad \Rightarrow \quad \frac{\partial z_h}{\partial w_{i \to h}} = x = 0.5\]
Gradient for \( w_{i \to h} \) ≈ −0.0086
Updated: \( w_{i \to h} = 0.4 + 0.5 \times 0.0086 \approx \mathbf{0.4043} \)

Example 2: \( x = 0.2 \), target \( t = 0.4 \)

Weights before this example: \( w_{i \to h} \approx 0.4043 \), \( w_{h_1 \to o} \approx 0.7272 \)

Forward Propagation (summary)

hidden_net ≈ 0.0809 → \( o_{h_1} \approx 0.5202 \)
output_net ≈ 0.3784 → \( o \approx 0.5935 \)
\( E \approx 0.0374 \)

Backpropagation — Both Weights

Gradient \( w_{h_1 \to o} \) ≈ +0.0484 → Updated to 0.7030
Gradient \( w_{i \to h} \) ≈ +0.0031 → Updated to 0.4028

Example 3: \( x = 0.8 \), target \( t = 0.9 \)

Weights before this example: \( w_{i \to h} \approx 0.4028 \), \( w_{h_1 \to o} \approx 0.7030 \)

Forward Propagation (summary)

hidden_net ≈ 0.3222 → \( o_{h_1} \approx 0.5799 \)
output_net ≈ 0.4077 → \( o \approx 0.6005 \)
\( E \approx 0.0897 \)

Backpropagation — Both Weights

Gradient \( w_{h_1 \to o} \) ≈ −0.0834 → Updated to 0.7447
Gradient \( w_{i \to h} \) ≈ −0.0109 → Updated to 0.4083

Final Weights After One Epoch

Weight Initial After Ex 1 After Ex 2 Final (After Ex 3)
\( w_{i \to h} \) 0.4000 0.4043 0.4028 0.4083
\( w_{h_1 \to o} \) 0.7000 0.7272 0.7030 0.7447

Key Points:

  • Both weights are updated after every single training example.
  • The gradient for \( w_{i \to h} \) is smaller because the error signal is multiplied by more terms as it travels backward.
  • This is the complete backpropagation algorithm on a small network.
